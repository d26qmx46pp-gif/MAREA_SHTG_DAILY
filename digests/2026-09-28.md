**Cas12a chRDNA-mediated in vivo genome editing for high specificity functional gene disruption**
Losa M, Rajmane P, Stanaway M, Coons M, Munoz A, Garish V, et al. *Molecular Therapy — Nucleic Acids*, 2026.
[PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42780917/) · [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.omtn.2026.103084)

The authors use "hybrid" CRISPR guides that mix RNA and DNA nucleotides (chRDNA). These guides have been shown to make Cas nucleases more specific in vitro, and here they are tested in vivo with Cas12a mRNA delivered by lipid nanoparticle. They targeted three clinically validated liver genes (Pcsk9, Angptl3 and Ttr). A single IV dose gave high editing efficiency with no detectable off-target editing, near-complete loss of each circulating protein in wild-type mice, and lower plasma cholesterol after the Pcsk9 and Angptl3 knockouts. High doses in rodents caused no liver toxicity, chronic cytokine release, or on-target editing outside the liver. This is preclinical mouse work from the platform's own developers. Still, it adds a third editing approach (after Cas9 nuclease and base editing) competing to make ANGPTL3 a one-time treatment, and the pitch rests on specificity.

**Early in-hospital triglyceride clearance trajectories and time-weighted mean exposure in hypertriglyceridemia-associated acute pancreatitis: associations with disease severity and pancreatic necrosis**
Cui J, Li J, Sun Z, Zhao J, Wu H. *Frontiers in Medicine*, 2026.
[PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42780899/) · [DOI](https://doi.org/10.3389/fmed.2026.1921788)

This is a retrospective cohort of 526 patients with HTG-associated acute pancreatitis, using serial triglyceride measurements from the first 7 days of admission. Trajectory modeling found two groups: a rapid-decline group (n=430) and a slow-decline group (n=96). Severe acute pancreatitis was far more common with slow decline (46.9% vs 11.9%; adjusted OR 6.44, 95% CI 3.55–11.69). Time-weighted mean TG exposure was also linked to severity (OR 1.66), while peak TG was not. Only time-weighted exposure was linked to pancreatic necrosis (OR 1.27). The takeaway is that sustained exposure matters more than the peak number, which argues for tracking TG clearance rather than the admission value. It is single-center and retrospective, so it needs validation.

**Healthy bone marrow transplantation into hypertriglyceridemic rats improves cardiac reparative fibrosis post-myocardial infarction**
Ramos-Regalado L, Alcover S, Padró T, Walker M, Gómez de Segura-Melcón G, Sancho-Pardo G, et al. *Cardiovascular Research*, 2026.
[PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42776037/) · [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1093/cvr/cvag204)

This study used crossed bone-marrow transplants in 56 Wistar rats, with hypertriglyceridemia induced by a high-sucrose diet, followed by transient coronary ligation. HTG recipients of normal marrow had smaller infarcts, a resolving inflammatory response, and better systolic and diastolic function, though LVEF did not differ between groups. Their circulating TG also fell. Going the other way, normal rats that received HTG marrow developed HTG, fatty liver, weight gain, and lower lipoprotein lipase activity in visceral fat. The provocative point is that marrow-derived cells seem to carry a transferable "HTG state" that includes lower adipose LPL activity. That is mechanistically interesting, but it is a diet-induced rat model with an irradiation confounder.

**Effect of pemafibrate on LDL-cholesterol properties in type 2 diabetes and hypertriglyceridemia by lipid management status: PARM-T2D post hoc analysis**
Kitsunai H, Kito K, Omori K, Cho KY, Nakamura A, Takeuchi J, et al. *Journal of Diabetes Investigation*, 2026.
[PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42788964/) · [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1111/jdi.70456)

This post hoc analysis covers 156 patients with type 2 diabetes and hypertriglyceridemia treated with pemafibrate for 52 weeks, grouped by baseline LDL-C. Triglycerides and remnant cholesterol improved in every group. LDL-C behaved differently depending on baseline. It rose without any rise in ApoB when baseline LDL-C was low, which suggests larger LDL particles. Both LDL-C and ApoB fell when baseline LDL-C was high. This is familiar fibrate/PPARα biology (TG lowering unmasking a rise in LDL-C), and the paper is a useful reminder to judge TG-lowering drugs by ApoB rather than LDL-C alone. It is a small post hoc analysis, so treat it as descriptive.

## Part B — Industry news (SHTG / Arrowhead / Alnylam / Ionis)

**Ionis-partnered ulefnersen succeeds in Phase 3 for FUS-ALS; Otsuka heads to regulators**
*BioPharma Dive*, Sept 24, 2026 (news roundup) — [article](https://www.biopharmadive.com/news/lilly-innocare-immunovant-vertex-inaxaplin-otsuka-ionis/831044/)
See also: *Fierce Biotech*, Sept 22, 2026 (Nick Paul Taylor) — [article](https://www.fiercebiotech.com/biotech/otsuka-heads-fda-ionis-partnered-als-drug-hits-primary-phase-3-endpoint) (the underlying announcement came Tuesday, Sept 22, so it sits at the edge of this cycle's window)

Otsuka reported that ulefnersen, an Ionis-designed antisense drug that lowers production of FUS protein, met its primary endpoint in the Phase 3 FUSION trial in ALS caused by FUS mutations. The primary endpoint was a combined measure of function and survival at Week 72, and the drug also hit key secondary endpoints, including a neurodegeneration biomarker and time to death, permanent ventilation, rescue, or withdrawal. The trial enrolled 89 patients (73 in the primary analysis), and safety was described as favorable. Otsuka, which paid Ionis $10 million upfront in 2024 for worldwide rights, plans to go to regulators. Commercial upside for Ionis is small: FUS-ALS is about 0.6% of ALS cases, TD Cowen called the value "incremental," and Guggenheim projects Ionis royalties of about $14 million by 2030. BioPharma Dive noted this was one of two late-stage wins that week for Ionis-partnered drugs; the other was Roche's sefaxersen, covered in the prior episode.

*Why it matters for SHTG / lipid programs:* This is not a lipid story. It is more evidence for the antisense platform and a small, welcome bit of good news for Ionis, whose main commercial story remains the Tryngolza sHTG launch.

No new SHTG, Arrowhead, or Alnylam stories surfaced in the allowed publications within the Sept 23–28 window. Arrowhead's plozasiran sHTG sNDA, which the company guided for before year-end 2026 with a priority review voucher, has not yet been reported as filed.

## What to watch

Both segments point the same way: the science is in a quiet stretch between readouts, and the next SHTG catalysts are commercial and regulatory. Watch for Arrowhead's plozasiran sHTG filing, which becomes a six-month review clock once the voucher is used, and Ionis's Q3 Tryngolza sHTG numbers. On the science side, the Cas12a hybrid-guide paper and last cycle's base-editing delivery work show one-time ANGPTL3 inactivation competing on specificity and delivery rather than on how much it lowers lipids. The HTG-pancreatitis study adds to the case that sustained TG exposure is what causes harm, which fits the durable-knockdown argument for APOC3 and ANGPTL3 drugs.
