## Part B — Industry news (SHTG / Arrowhead / Alnylam / Ionis)

**Alnylam plunges 30% as Q2 earnings deliver a "one-two punch" on ATTR guidance**
BioPharma Dive, July 30, 2026 — https://www.biopharmadive.com/news/alnylam-earnings-stock-amvuttra-onpattro-sales-pfizer-ionis/826654/
(See also: Fierce Pharma, July 30, 2026, "Alnylam shares tank 29% as Amvuttra disappoints with ATTR outlook cut for 2026" — https://www.fiercepharma.com/pharma/alnylam-shares-tank-27-amvuttra-disappoints-attr-outlook-cut-2026)

Alnylam Pharmaceuticals lost close to $12 billion in market value Thursday after reporting second-quarter 2026 results that missed Wall Street's expectations for its lead RNAi drug, Amvuttra. Amvuttra crossed $1 billion in quarterly sales for the first time ($1.01 billion, including $809 million in the U.S.), but that came in about 4% below consensus. Management said the shortfall reflects "pent-up demand" from Amvuttra's 2025 launch normalizing rather than any new safety or efficacy signal. In response, Alnylam cut its full-year total ATTR revenue guidance by $200 million at both ends, to a range of $4.2–4.5 billion. CEO Yvonne Greenstreet and commercial chief Tolga Tanguler pushed back on the bearish read, citing greater than 90% patient adherence and continued strength in first-line prescribing, but analysts at Cantor Fitzgerald, Oppenheimer, and Jefferies all flagged the guidance cut as an unwelcome surprise given otherwise-solid prescription trends. The reaction was compounded by a separate, already-known setback earlier in July in which an AstraZeneca/Ionis ATTR-CM rival therapy failed a pivotal trial — a result that had been expected to help rather than hurt Alnylam's competitive position, adding to the "one-two punch" framing from sell-side analysts.

*Why it matters for SHTG / lipid programs:* This story isn't about triglycerides directly — it's about the ATTR business — but it's a useful gauge of investor patience with RNAi/ASO platform companies broadly, including Ionis, right as both companies also carry lipid-lowering franchises (Ionis's Tryngolza in sHTG, Alnylam's earlier-stage APOC3/ANGPTL3 pipeline interest). Volatility here can affect capital available for lipid program investment and how Street analysts price competitive dynamics between Ionis's Tryngolza and Arrowhead's Redemplo going forward.
