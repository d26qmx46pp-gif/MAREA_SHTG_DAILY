## Part A — SHTG / lipid science (PubMed)

**Expectations for Angiopoietin-like 3 Inhibitors Taking Broad Lipid-modifying Effects on the Comprehensive Control of Atherosclerotic Cardiovascular Disease Risk in Patients with Homozygous Familial Hypercholesterolemia**
Yoshida H. *Journal of Atherosclerosis and Thrombosis*, Sep 11, 2026 (ahead of print). [PMID 42732948](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42732948/) | [DOI](https://doi.org/10.5551/jat.ED314)
This is a single-author editorial (no structured abstract in the record) rather than a primary study. It argues that ANGPTL3 inhibitors — which lower LDL-C, triglycerides, and HDL-C simultaneously by restoring lipoprotein lipase and endothelial lipase activity — offer a distinct advantage over LDL-selective therapies in homozygous familial hypercholesterolemia (HoFH), where residual triglyceride-rich lipoprotein burden also drives cardiovascular risk. The piece frames ANGPTL3 blockade (already in clinical use as evinacumab, with RNAi/siRNA candidates in development) as a tool for "comprehensive" atherosclerotic risk control rather than LDL-C alone. Being an editorial, it's a synthesis/opinion piece rather than new data — useful context for how the field is framing ANGPTL3's broader relevance beyond triglycerides, but not a trial result.

**Clinical heterogeneity and disease progression during adolescence in AGPAT2-associated congenital generalized lipodystrophy: a case series of four patients**
*Journal of Pediatric Endocrinology & Metabolism*, Sep 14, 2026 (ahead of print). [PMID 42731057](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42731057/) | [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1515/jpem-2026-0516)
A case series following four genetically confirmed AGPAT2-associated congenital generalized lipodystrophy (CGL1) patients through adolescence. AGPAT2 loss disrupts triglyceride synthesis in adipocytes, producing near-total lipoatrophy and, commonly, severe hypertriglyceridemia as fat is redirected into ectopic and circulating lipid pools. Despite all four patients sharing the disease-causing genotype (and in some cases being siblings with the same homozygous variant), the authors report markedly variable metabolic severity — generalized lipoatrophy, muscular hypertrophy, acromegaloid features, and hyperphagia were shared, but complications diverged. The takeaway is that genotype alone poorly predicts metabolic trajectory in this rare severe-HTG-associated lipodystrophy, arguing for individualized longitudinal monitoring through puberty.

**Dietary fatty acids modulate lipolytic enzymes via distinct allosteric and exosite mechanisms: an integrated in vivo and in silico study linking cafeteria diet-induced obesity to altered lipid metabolism**
*Biochemical and Biophysical Research Communications*, Sep 2026 (ahead of print). [PMID 42570490](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42570490/) | [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.bbrc.2026.154397)
Wistar rats fed a hypercaloric "cafeteria" diet for eight weeks were compared to standard-diet controls, with tissue lipid content and lipolytic enzyme activity assessed alongside molecular docking simulations. The study specifically probes how dietary fatty acids modulate lipoprotein lipase (LPL) and hormone-sensitive lipase (HSL) through separate mechanisms — allosteric modulation for one enzyme, exosite (surface-binding-site) interactions for the other — rather than a single shared pathway. It's a mechanistic diet-obesity study, not a human or SHTG-specific trial, but it adds molecular detail to how chronic high-fat feeding could blunt LPL-mediated triglyceride clearance, one of the core pathways therapeutics like olezarsen and plozasiran work to bypass or amplify.
