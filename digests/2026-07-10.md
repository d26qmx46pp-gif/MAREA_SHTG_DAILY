## Part A — SHTG / lipid science (PubMed)

**Olezarsen and Plozasiran: Novel apoC-III targeting medications for the treatment of hypertriglyceridemia.** Kelly MS, Lewis J, Zuch D, Rogowsky MR, Dixon DL. *Journal of Cardiovascular Pharmacology*, July 6, 2026. [PubMed 42407025](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42407025/) | [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1097/FJC.0000000000001854)
This is a clinician-facing review rather than new data, but it's a useful state-of-play summary of the two approved/near-approved apoC-III–lowering drugs. Both olezarsen (Ionis) and plozasiran (Arrowhead) inhibit hepatic apoC-III mRNA production, which increases triglyceride clearance; both have shown significant TG-lowering and favorable effects on other lipid parameters in randomized, placebo-controlled trials in FCS and hypertriglyceridemia. The authors flag routine monitoring needs — liver enzymes and glycemic control for both agents, plus platelet counts specifically for olezarsen. They note the open question the field still needs answered: whether TG-lowering from either drug actually translates into fewer pancreatitis and ASCVD events, not just fewer lab abnormalities.

**A prognostic model based on metabolic indicators for recurrent acute pancreatitis in hypertriglyceridemia patients: a retrospective cohort study.** Wang Z, Liu Y, Ren P, et al. *Annals of Medicine*, 2026 (electronic ahead of print April 2026, indexed this cycle). [PubMed 41978916](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41978916/) | [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1080/07853890.2026.2653904)
A 293-patient retrospective cohort out of Shandong found that 21.5% of hypertriglyceridemic acute pancreatitis (HTG-AP) patients had a recurrence within two years. Visceral adipose tissue index, triglyceride-glucose index, smoking, and drinking status independently predicted recurrence, and a nomogram built from these variables hit an AUC of 0.927 — notably better than any single predictor. It's a single-center retrospective design, so it needs external validation, but it's a reminder that recurrence risk in HTG-AP is not just about peak triglyceride level; metabolic burden and behavioral factors matter just as much, which is relevant context for how aggressively to treat and monitor patients after a first HTG-AP episode.

**Retinochoroidal sequelae following resolution of lipemia retinalis in an infant with compound heterozygous LPL variants.** Martinuzzi D, Pignatto S, Menchini F, Lanzetta P. *Journal of AAPOS*, July 9, 2026. [PubMed 42425440](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42425440/) | [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jaapos.2026.104892)
A case report of a 20-day-old girl with compound heterozygous LPL variants (p.Trp113Arg / p.Met328Ile) who presented with severe hypertriglyceridemia and stage 3 lipemia retinalis. Despite biochemical normalization after fasting, IV fluids, and exchange transfusion, follow-up fluorescein angiography showed persistent patchy choroidal filling delay and focal hyperfluorescent spots — evidence of lasting retinal pigment epithelium injury. Lipemia retinalis is usually taught as a benign, fully reversible sign of severe hypertriglyceridemia; this case pushes back on that a little, suggesting genetic LPL deficiency in infancy may leave subtle structural eye damage even after triglycerides normalize.

## Part B — Industry news (SHTG / Arrowhead / Alnylam / Ionis)

**AZ, Ionis shares tumble on ATTR-CM trial flop, but analyst flags over-reaction.** Fierce Pharma, July 9, 2026, by Kevin Dunleavy. [Source](https://www.fiercepharma.com/pharma/az-ionis-shares-tumble-attr-cm-trial-failure-it-over-reaction)
Not a lipid-program story, but a major Ionis pipeline event worth flagging given the show's company coverage. AstraZeneca and Ionis' Phase 3 CARDIO-TTRansform trial of Wainua (eplontersen) in transthyretin-mediated amyloid cardiomyopathy (ATTR-CM) missed its primary endpoint — reduction in cardiovascular deaths or recurrent cardiovascular events through 140 weeks — in 1,432 patients. AZ shares fell 7% and Ionis fell 20%, while ATTR-CM rivals Alnylam (Amvuttra) and BridgeBio (Attruby) rose 10% and 15% respectively on the news, since Wainua's stumble effectively removes a would-be challenger from their market. A prespecified monotherapy subgroup did show nominal benefit, and Jefferies analysts called the market reaction an overreaction given limited impact on AZ's 2030 sales targets, but William Blair's Myles Minter noted the miss is a genuine surprise given how well RNAi silencers like Amvuttra have performed in the same indication.
*Why it matters for SHTG / lipid programs:* No direct lipid read-through, but it's a reminder of how binary Phase 3 cardiovascular outcome trials can be for these companies, and it strengthens Alnylam's relative competitive position in cardiometabolic RNA therapeutics generally, even outside the lipid franchise.

## What to watch

The Wainua miss doesn't touch the SHTG franchise directly, but it's a useful gut-check on trial risk just as Arrowhead's SHASTA-3/4/5 plozasiran trials in severe hypertriglyceridemia head toward a planned 2026 sNDA filing and Ionis works to convert Tryngolza's new sHTG label into real-world uptake. On the literature side, keep an eye out for prospective, multi-center data on HTG-AP recurrence prediction (the Shandong nomogram needs external validation) and any longer-term follow-up on retinal outcomes in genetic LPL deficiency, since the AAPOS case report suggests "benign" lipemia retinalis may deserve closer ophthalmologic follow-up than it currently gets.
