## Part A — SHTG / lipid science (PubMed)

No papers from the target top-tier journal list (NEJM, Lancet, JAMA, Nature/Nat Med, Circulation, JACC, Eur Heart J, JCI, Cell Metab, Nat Metab, Circ Res, Lancet Diabetes Endocrinol, etc.) matched the search terms in the last 5 days. Broadening to all PubMed-indexed journals surfaced 48 hits; after removing papers already covered in recent episodes, five next-best-tier items stood out:

**Clinical and functional evidence supporting pathogenicity of a novel APOA5 variant in familial chylomicronemia syndrome.** Gutiérrez J, Castaño P, Monsalve C, et al. *Archives of Endocrinology and Metabolism.* [PMID 42721061](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42721061/) · [DOI](https://doi.org/10.20945/2359-4292-2026-0101). Ten patients with FCS carried a homozygous APOA5 missense variant (c.694T>C; p.Ser232Pro) of previously uncertain significance. Post-heparin LPL activity was under 20% of normal in affected patients, and adding back exogenous APOA5 restored LPL activity above 20% in every case — direct functional evidence that this variant impairs LPL function. The authors argue this supports reclassifying the variant as likely pathogenic and helps distinguish monogenic FCS from multifactorial chylomicronemia, which matters for how aggressively a patient's triglycerides get treated.

**Fresh frozen plasma as functional replacement therapy in early infantile ApoC-II deficiency: a molecularly confirmed case series.** Üçüncü Ergun N, Aslantaş M. *Journal of Pediatric Endocrinology and Metabolism.* [PMID 42148757](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42148757/) · [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1515/jpem-2026-0123). Three male infants from consanguineous families presented with extreme triglycerides (3,228–5,802 mg/dL) from homozygous ApoC-II deficiency. Fresh frozen plasma infusion supplied functional ApoC-II and rapidly stabilized triglycerides, doubling as both treatment and a diagnostic bridge while genetic confirmation and long-term dietary management were arranged. A reminder that ApoC-II, the LPL cofactor, remains a distinct — and treatable in a pinch — cause of infantile SHTG.

**Comparative Efficacy of Insulin and Alternative Therapies for Hypertriglyceridemia-Associated Acute Pancreatitis: A Systematic Review and Network Meta-Analysis.** Chen Liang A, Cruz Castillo Y, Murrieta Bruciaga LM, et al. *Digestive Diseases and Sciences.* [PMID 42301369](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42301369/) · [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1007/s10620-026-10052-2). A network meta-analysis of mostly observational studies comparing insulin, therapeutic plasma exchange, heparin, and hemofiltration for HTG-induced pancreatitis. In RCT-restricted analyses, plasma exchange dropped triglycerides faster than insulin and insulin-plus-heparin shortened hospital stay, but faster biochemical triglyceride lowering did not translate into lower mortality, organ failure, or shorter stays overall. Bottom line for practice: no acute intervention has convincingly beaten insulin on outcomes that matter, and the field still needs adequately powered RCTs.

***Previously covered — new context this cycle:* Trimming the Fat: How Olezarsen Is Poised to Transform the Management of Severe Hypertriglyceridemia-Induced Acute Pancreatitis.** Khalil I, Hossain MI, Khalid AA, Sarker P. *Digestive Diseases and Sciences.* [PMID 42213381](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42213381/) · [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1007/s10620-026-10019-3). A commentary/perspective on olezarsen's role in SHTG-associated pancreatitis; no abstract was available via PubMed, so treat this as a pointer to a discussion piece rather than new primary data — worth a look given how directly it engages with Ionis's approved indication.

***Previously covered — new context this cycle:* Zodasiran in mixed hyperlipidaemia: durable lipid lowering, cardiovascular benefit unresolved.** Ahmadizar F, Hollander M. *European Journal of Preventive Cardiology.* [PMID 42709415](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42709415/) · [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1093/eurjpc/zwag485). Another commentary-style entry with no abstract text indexed; the title signals a critical read on Arrowhead's ANGPTL3-lowering zodasiran — durable lipid effects appear well established, but whether that translates to cardiovascular outcome benefit is flagged as still open. Worth pulling the full text given the direct relevance to Arrowhead's lipid franchise.
