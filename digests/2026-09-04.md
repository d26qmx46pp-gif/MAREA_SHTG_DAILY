## Part A — SHTG / lipid science (PubMed)

**A Case of Familial Chylomicronemia Syndrome Caused by a Novel Homozygous GPIHBP1 Mutation Successfully Treated with the Selective PPARα Modulator Pemafibrate.**
Otsuki T, Chung Y, Kawachi Y, et al. *Journal of Atherosclerosis and Thrombosis*, Sep 2026. [PMID: 42309774](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42309774/) | [DOI: 10.5551/jat.66218](https://doi.org/10.5551/jat.66218)

A 47-year-old woman with childhood-diagnosed FCS presented with acute pancreatitis and was found to carry a novel homozygous frameshift mutation in GPIHBP1 (the LPL-anchoring protein on capillary endothelium), with undetectable circulating GPIHBP1 and markedly reduced preheparin LPL mass. Notably, both of her pregnancies were major triglyceride-elevation triggers — one complicated by pancreatitis, the other reaching TG >6,000 mg/dL without pancreatitis. She was managed with strict fat restriction plus the selective PPARα modulator pemafibrate, which held TG below 500 mg/dL on follow-up. GPIHBP1 deficiency is an exceedingly rare cause of FCS distinct from LPL, apoC-II, apoA-V, or LMF1 defects, and this case adds a genotype to the literature while illustrating pemafibrate as a viable chronic management option once acute pancreatitis resolves.

**Fenofibrate vs. Omega-3 in Pediatric Hypertriglyceridemia: A Randomized Controlled Trial.**
Dalili S, Koohmanaee S, Hassanzadeh Rad A, et al. *Journal of Pediatric Health Care*. [PMID: 41636674](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41636674/) | [DOI: 10.1016/j.pedhc.2025.12.021](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.pedhc.2025.12.021)

An open-label RCT out of Rasht, Iran randomized 30 children/adolescents (ages 8–18) with TG >400 mg/dL, or >250 mg/dL unresponsive to lifestyle measures, to fenofibrate 100 mg daily versus omega-3 1,000 mg twice daily for 12 weeks. Fenofibrate produced significantly larger reductions in triglycerides, LDL-C, and total cholesterol than omega-3, while omega-3 modestly raised HDL-C. Small sample and open-label design limit strength of inference, but it's one of the few randomized comparisons of these two options specifically in pediatric HTG, a population with sparse trial data.
