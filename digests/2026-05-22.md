## Part A — SHTG / lipid science (PubMed)

*Top-tier journals, last 7 days. Three papers met the bar this cycle.*

**Effect of APOC3 Inhibition With Olezarsen on Coronary Atherosclerosis: Essence-TIMI 73b Imaging Study**

Marston NA, Bergmark BA, Prohaska TA, Moura FA, Zimerman A, et al. *Circulation*, May 19 2026. PMID: [41910513](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41910513/) | DOI: [10.1161/CIRCULATIONAHA.126.080012](https://doi.org/10.1161/CIRCULATIONAHA.126.080012)

This is the most consequential SHTG paper of the week. Within the randomized, placebo-controlled Essence-TIMI 73b trial, 468 patients with largely moderate hypertriglyceridemia (median TG 249 mg/dL at baseline) underwent coronary CT angiography at baseline and 12 months. Olezarsen produced robust lipid changes: at 6 months it reduced triglycerides by 63.9%, remnant cholesterol by 71.9%, and ApoB by 16%, with no effect on LDL-C — exactly the pharmacology expected from an APOC3 inhibitor. Despite those reductions, the primary endpoint — percent change in noncalcified coronary plaque volume — did not differ between olezarsen and placebo (placebo-adjusted difference 2.98%, 95% CI −3.4 to 9.3; p=0.36). No significant differences were seen for low-attenuation, calcified, or total plaque volume either.

The finding is notable for two reasons. First, it challenges the hypothesis that lowering triglyceride-rich lipoproteins and remnant cholesterol at the levels achievable with APOC3 ASO therapy is sufficient to regress established coronary plaque within 12 months — at least in patients with moderate (not severe) hypertriglyceridemia. Second, it underscores that the primary benefit case for olezarsen and its class likely rests on pancreatitis prevention and longer-term cardiovascular outcomes data, not near-term plaque modification. Whether longer treatment duration, higher-risk TG ranges, or imaging endpoints like low-attenuation plaque would have yielded different results remains open.

**2026 ACC/AHA/AACVPR/ABC/ACPM/ADA/AGS/APhA/ASPC/NLA/PCNA Guideline on the Management of Dyslipidemia**

Blumenthal RS, Morris PB, Gaudino M, Johnson HM, Anderson TS, et al. *Journal of the American College of Cardiology*, May 19 2026. PMID: [41824590](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41824590/) | DOI: [10.1016/j.jacc.2025.11.016](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jacc.2025.11.016)

The first full revision to the U.S. dyslipidemia practice guidelines since 2018 was published this week — a major event for the field. This document formally retires the 2018 ACC/AHA blood cholesterol guideline and incorporates a far broader scope: it explicitly addresses the evaluation, management, and monitoring of high cholesterol, **hypertriglyceridemia**, and elevated lipoprotein(a). The inclusion of hypertriglyceridemia as a co-equal section alongside LDL-C reflects the accumulating evidence base for APOC3 and ANGPTL3 inhibition, including data from the Core/Core2 trials for olezarsen in sHTG and plozasiran's FCS program. The full text and specific TG/SHTG recommendations are not yet parsed in PubMed's abstract record; formal commentary from SHTG experts is anticipated over the coming weeks. This guideline update will significantly shape clinical practice around triglyceride thresholds, treatment targets, and the role of RNA-targeted therapies in the dyslipidemia algorithm.

**Triglycerides and Residual Risk, Where Do They Fit Now?**

Nordestgaard BG. *Journal of the American College of Cardiology*, May 19 2026. PMID: [42153695](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42153695/) | DOI: [10.1016/j.jacc.2026.02.5133](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jacc.2026.02.5133)

This is an invited editorial or perspective by Børge Nordestgaard — one of the most prolific researchers in triglyceride and remnant-cholesterol epidemiology — published alongside the Essence-TIMI 73b imaging study and the new dyslipidemia guideline. PubMed does not carry the full abstract, but the framing of the title says a great deal: "Where do they fit now?" directly references the unresolved debate about whether triglycerides are causal for ASCVD or merely a marker, and how to reconcile mechanistic and genetic evidence for causality against negative or null imaging trial results. Nordestgaard's editorials in JACC are typically concise frameworks for the field; this one should be required reading for anyone interpreting the Essence-TIMI 73b null result in the context of the new guideline's TG-lowering recommendations.

**Ionis Q1 2026 Earnings: Olezarsen sNDA Accepted for Priority Review; PDUFA Date June 30, 2026 — context piece, April 29, 2026**

Ionis Pharmaceuticals, Inc. Earnings Release. Source: [SEC Form 8-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0000874015/000114036126017637/ef20071749_ex99-1.htm)

At its Q1 2026 earnings call on April 29, Ionis reported that the FDA has accepted the supplemental NDA for olezarsen (Tryngolza) in severe hypertriglyceridemia for Priority Review, with a PDUFA target action date of June 30, 2026. The company simultaneously raised its annual peak net sales guidance for olezarsen in sHTG from >$2 billion to >$3 billion — a significant vote of confidence in what would be Ionis' first large-population commercial franchise. First-quarter TRYNGOLZA sales for FCS came in at $27 million, with the EU launch via Sobi underway. The EMA has also accepted an indication extension application for olezarsen in sHTG, filed in March. If the FDA approval comes through on or near the PDUFA date, Ionis would be poised to launch olezarsen in the much larger sHTG market this summer.

*Why it matters for SHTG / lipid programs:* A June 30 PDUFA date means a potential U.S. approval decision is now less than six weeks away — the single most consequential near-term regulatory event in the SHTG space and a pivotal moment for RNA-targeted lipid therapy broadly.

**Arrowhead Q2 FY2026 Update: REDEMPLO Hits 400+ Prescriptions, EU Approval Imminent, Dual siRNA ARO-DIMER-PA Enters Clinic — context piece, May 7, 2026**

Arrowhead Pharmaceuticals Earnings Release. Source: [SEC Form 8-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0000879407/000087940726000045/arwr-202605078kex991.htm)

On May 7, Arrowhead reported that REDEMPLO (plozasiran) has crossed 400 total prescriptions since its November 2025 FDA approval for FCS, with roughly 180 patients having received at least one injection and new weekly prescriptions now averaging ~30 per week — representing >40% prescription growth over the prior four weeks. The company updated REDEMPLO's U.S. WAC to $45,000 per patient per year, explicitly designed as a unified price that will hold if the SHTG indication is approved, simplifying formulary negotiations. On the regulatory front, a positive CHMP opinion recommending EU approval has been received; the European Commission decision is expected in Q2 2026. Arrowhead also announced the first patients dosed in a Phase 1/2a trial of ARO-DIMER-PA, the first dual-targeting siRNA in clinical development — a single molecule targeting both PCSK9 and APOC3 simultaneously — and disclosed a licensing deal with Madrigal Pharmaceuticals for ARO-PNPLA3 ($25M upfront, up to $1B in milestones) and a $930M capital raise.

*Why it matters for SHTG / lipid programs:* The ARO-DIMER-PA clinical entry is a meaningful platform milestone — simultaneously silencing PCSK9 and APOC3 in one molecule could deliver both LDL-C and TG/remnant reduction, addressing the residual risk gap that the Essence-TIMI 73b null imaging result this week makes even more salient.

## What to watch

The biggest event on the horizon for this field is now measured in weeks, not months: Ionis' June 30 PDUFA date for olezarsen in sHTG. If approved, it would be the first RNA-targeted medicine indicated for a broad population of patients with elevated triglycerides — a watershed commercial and clinical moment. This week's negative Essence-TIMI 73b plaque imaging data will almost certainly feature prominently in the FDA's benefit-risk calculus and in the label discussions around cardiovascular outcomes; the pancreatitis prevention data from Core/Core2 remain the primary evidence pillar. The simultaneously released 2026 ACC/AHA Dyslipidemia Guideline will determine how broadly cardiologists and endocrinologists reach for TG-lowering therapies once (and if) an sHTG indication lands. For Arrowhead, the EU Commission's impending REDEMPLO approval decision closes a quarter where the company established a clear global commercial footprint in FCS. Longer term, Arrowhead's ARO-DIMER-PA — and the open question of whether combined PCSK9+APOC3 silencing can achieve coronary plaque regression where APOC3 inhibition alone did not — is the most scientifically provocative program to watch across the RNAi lipid space.
