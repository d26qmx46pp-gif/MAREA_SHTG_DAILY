## Part A — SHTG / lipid science (PubMed)

No top-tier journal hits this cycle. Broadened the query (dropped journal filter) per the SKILL's fallback rule and selected the four most relevant papers — flagging this clearly as the next-best tier.

**Efficacy and Safety of Olezarsen in Hypertriglyceridemia: An Updated Meta-Analysis of Randomised Controlled Trials**
Ahmed Emara, Ahmed Mansour, et al. *Diabetes, Obesity & Metabolism*, June 2026.
[PMID 41943994](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41943994/)
Updated meta-analysis pooling olezarsen RCTs in hypertriglyceridemia. Confirms a robust triglyceride-lowering effect on top of standard lipid therapy and an acute pancreatitis benefit — the dominant safety signal that's driving the case for sHTG label expansion. Most directly relevant Part A reading for anyone tracking Ionis' Tryngolza pivotal program ahead of the June 30 PDUFA window.

**Efficacy and Safety of Apolipoprotein C-III Inhibitors in Hypertriglyceridemia: A Network Meta-Analysis of Randomised Controlled Trials**
Mohammed A Elbahloul, Aliaa Gamal, et al. *Diabetes, Obesity & Metabolism*, June 2026.
[PMID 41917770](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41917770/)
Network meta-analysis of apoC-III inhibitors as a class — i.e., putting olezarsen (Ionis ASO) and plozasiran (Arrowhead siRNA, also indirectly via apoC-III biology) on the same playing field in hypertriglyceridemia. The class effect on triglycerides is large and durable; the head-to-head differentiation will continue to come down to dosing schedule, route, and pancreatitis-prevention durability.

**RNA interference for lipid disorders: is this the future?**
Miguel Nassif, Renata Turrini Jacob Fazoli, et al. *Current Opinion in Lipidology*, June 2026.
[PMID 41762752](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41762752/)
Timely review framing the current state of RNAi in lipid management — covers PCSK9 (inclisiran), apoC-III, ANGPTL3, and Lp(a) programs. Useful if you want a single piece to hand to someone trying to get oriented in the Arrowhead vs. Alnylam vs. Ionis landscape; the authors are bullish on RNAi durability vs. ASO chemistry, particularly for chronic indications where quarterly dosing matters.

**A prognostic model based on metabolic indicators for recurrent acute pancreatitis in hypertriglyceridemia patients: a retrospective cohort study**
Zhiguo Wang, Yongshuai Liu, et al. *Annals of Medicine*, December 2026 (e-publication).
[PMID 41978916](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41978916/)
Single-center retrospective cohort developing a metabolic-marker model to predict recurrence of HTG-acute pancreatitis. Modest cohort and external validation will be needed, but the topic is a direct match for the population that pivotal sHTG trials are now targeting — and a reminder that even with effective lipid-lowering, recurrence biology is a wide-open clinical and commercial question.
