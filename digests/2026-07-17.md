## Part A — SHTG / lipid science (PubMed)

**A quarter-century of progress: innovations in lipid medicine that transformed cardiovascular prevention**
Lluís Masana, Josep Ribalta, Daiana Ibarretxe. *Lancet Diabetes & Endocrinology* (Aug 2026 issue; indexed July 15, 2026). [PMID: 42259344](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42259344/) | [DOI: 10.1016/S2213-8587(26)00102-6](https://doi.org/10.1016/S2213-8587(26)00102-6)

This is a "TimeCapsule" review rather than a primary research report, so treat it as context, not new data. The authors trace 25 years of lipid-medicine advances that reshaped cardiovascular prevention: the consolidation of statins as foundational therapy, the emergence of non-statin agents, and — most relevant here — the arrival of RNA- and DNA-targeted therapies capable of modulating PCSK9, lipoprotein(a), apolipoprotein C-III, and ANGPTL3. They also flag the growing embrace of non-HDL-cholesterol, apoB, and remnant cholesterol as residual-risk metrics, alongside a persistent evidence-practice gap in getting patients to target. No new trial data on SHTG, ANGPTL3/4, APOC3, or LPL specifically — it's a framing piece situating those targets within the broader arc of lipid drug development.
