**A prognostic model based on metabolic indicators for recurrent acute pancreatitis in hypertriglyceridemia patients: a retrospective cohort study.** Wang Z, Liu Y, Ren P, Zhu G, Zhang G, Zhang X, et al. *Annals of Medicine.* [PMID: 41978916](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41978916/) | [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1186/s13017-025-00601-x)

This retrospective cohort from a single Chinese hospital followed 293 patients treated for hypertriglyceridemia-induced acute pancreatitis (HTG-AP) between 2019 and 2023. Over two years of follow-up, 63 patients (21.5%) had a recurrence. The authors used Cox regression to identify predictors — visceral adipose tissue index came out as an independent risk factor — and built a nomogram to stratify recurrence risk, validated internally by bootstrapping and cross-validation. It's a modest, single-center study rather than a practice-changing trial, but it's directly relevant to a persistent clinical question in SHTG management: which patients need the most aggressive triglyceride-lowering and follow-up after an HTG-AP episode. Worth a skim rather than a deep read given the retrospective, single-site design.
