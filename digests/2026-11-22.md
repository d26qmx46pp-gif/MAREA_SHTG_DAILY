## Part A — SHTG / lipid science (PubMed)

*No top-tier hits this week. Showing the next-best tier.* The 5-day top-journal query returned zero papers, so the journal filter was dropped and the papers below were picked from the broader results.

**1. The Role of Angiopoietin-Like Proteins 3 and 4 and Their Target Lipases in CKD: A Drug-Target Mendelian Randomization Study**
Yang G, Zhang DY, Fischman CJ, … Cuchel M, … Rader DJ. *Am J Kidney Dis.* 2026.
[PMID 42772348](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42772348/) · [DOI 10.1053/j.ajkd.2026.06.015](https://doi.org/10.1053/j.ajkd.2026.06.015)
This study used genetic proxies for ANGPTL3, ANGPTL4, LPL, endothelial lipase and hepatic lipase in about 2.37 million people (186,388 CKD cases) from All of Us, the Penn Medicine Biobank and GWAS consortia, across European, East Asian and African ancestries. Per 1-SD drop in triglycerides, genetically proxied ANGPTL3 inhibition (OR 0.76), ANGPTL4 inhibition (OR 0.78) and LPL enhancement (OR 0.86) were each linked to lower CKD risk and higher eGFR. Endothelial lipase and hepatic lipase signals depended on ancestry. Single-cell data showed that ANGPTL3, ANGPTL4 and LPL are expressed differently across kidney cell types and between CKD patients and controls. This is human genetic support for a kidney-protective benefit from the TG-lowering axis, which matters for SHTG populations where CKD is common.

**2. Lipoprotein lipase requires a flexible lid and stable C terminus maintained by ApoC-II peptide binding**
Lietzke EE, Rouse MS, Oldham D, Dong Z, … Bruce KD. *Cell Rep Phys Sci.* 2026.
[PMID 43054997](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/43054997/) · [DOI 10.1016/j.xcrp.2026.103562](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.xcrp.2026.103562)
The authors ran large molecular dynamics simulations of full-length LPL with a predicted lid domain, both alone and bound to an apoC-II mimetic peptide. The lid takes several orientations that control access to the lipid pore and catalytic activity, and the C-terminal domain can flip about 180°. The apoC-II peptide bridges the N- and C-terminal domains, which stabilizes productive conformations while keeping the lid flexible. In biochemical and cell assays the peptide clearly increased LPL-mediated TG hydrolysis. This is mechanistic support for apoC-II mimetics as LPL activators.

**3. Translating clinical trial efficacy data into population-level impact: a microsimulation model to compare APOC3 RNA inhibition on lowering triglycerides**
Desai NR, Bognar K, Speller A, Das AK. *J Comp Eff Res.* 2026.
[PMID 43048772](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/43048772/) · [DOI 10.57264/cer-2026-0095](https://doi.org/10.57264/cer-2026-0095)
This is a Monte Carlo patient-level simulation that indirectly compares plozasiran (from PALISADE) with olezarsen (from BALANCE) in FCS. In the base case, 56.8% of simulated plozasiran patients reached TG <500 mg/dL at 10 months, versus 18.2% on olezarsen. The direction held in all three scenario analyses, including matching the cohort to BALANCE or to genetically confirmed FCS. Treat it with caution: it is a model built on cross-trial efficacy distributions, not a head-to-head trial. The abstract does not state funding, so check the conflict-of-interest disclosures before citing it competitively.

**4. APOA5 frameshift mutation S78fs induces partial nonsense-mediated mRNA decay and predisposition to severe hypertriglyceridemia**
Wang L, Li J, He H, Jiang L, … Wang G. *Hum Genomics.* 2026.
[PMID 43058921](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/43058921/) · [DOI 10.1186/s40246-026-01029-3](https://doi.org/10.1186/s40246-026-01029-3)
In a family with fibrate-resistant SHTG, a heterozygous APOA5 c.230dupT (p.Ser78GlufsTer190) variant was the only monogenic predisposition that tracked with disease. Functional work showed that about 60% of mutant transcripts were degraded by nonsense-mediated decay, and the rest made a C-terminally truncated protein. Carriers' TG rose sharply in response to common metabolic stressors, which fits the "multifactorial chylomicronemia with a strong single-gene hit" picture.

**5. Cardiovascular Outcomes of Triglyceride-Targeting Lipid-Lowering Therapies in Patients with CKD**
Naim MAAZ, Thomas F, Streja E, Davis RL, … Kovesdy CP. *Kidney Int Rep.* 2026.
[PMID 43022087](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/43022087/) · [DOI 10.1016/j.ekir.2026.107116](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ekir.2026.107116)
This target-trial emulation in 46,904 US veterans with new CKD compared people starting a fibrate, niacin or a statin. New fibrate users had a modestly lower MACE risk than new statin users (HR 0.93, 0.86–0.99), driven mostly by heart failure (HR 0.87). Niacin showed no difference. Residual confounding in an observational comparison is a real limitation, but the paper pairs naturally with the MR study above on the TG–kidney axis.

**FDA approves Regeneron's Livsayis (cemdisiran), an Alnylam-discovered siRNA, for generalized myasthenia gravis**
BioPharma Dive (Ben Fidler), Nov 19, 2026. [Link](https://www.biopharmadive.com/news/regeneron-fda-approve-cemdisiran-livsayis-myasthenia-gravis-alnylam/875160/)
On Nov 18 the FDA approved Livsayis, a liver-targeted siRNA that silences complement C5, for adults with AChR antibody-positive generalized myasthenia gravis. It is given subcutaneously four times a year. Alnylam discovered the drug, Regeneron took monotherapy rights in 2024 and handled development, and the approval rests on the Phase 3 NIMBLE trial (2.3-point placebo-adjusted MG-ADL improvement at 24 weeks, per Fierce). Alnylam receives a $25M milestone and tiered royalties, which Fierce reports as up to 15%. It is the seventh FDA-approved drug from Alnylam's platform. List price is $410,000 a year. Analysts disagree on the launch: RBC expects "measured" uptake, while TD Cowen projects $1.5B at peak.
*Why it matters for SHTG / lipid programs:* This is the first RNAi approval in an autoimmune disease, and the quarterly hepatocyte-siRNA model is now showing up well outside rare liver disease. It adds to the evidence on durability and acceptance for the same delivery platform behind plozasiran and zodasiran.

## What to watch

The genetic data in Part A keep stacking up in the same direction: lowering TG through the ANGPTL3/4–LPL axis looks kidney-protective as well as pancreas- and possibly heart-protective. That could become part of the case for SHTG therapies beyond pancreatitis prevention. On the commercial side, the plozasiran-versus-olezarsen fight in SHTG is the main story to track. The modeling paper this week is an early sign of the comparative-effectiveness arguments both sides will make to payers. Arrowhead's fiscal-year results, which usually come out in late November, and any update on its sHTG filing are the next likely catalysts.
