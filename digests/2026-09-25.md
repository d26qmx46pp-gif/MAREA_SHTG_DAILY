## Part A — SHTG / lipid science (PubMed)

*Note: no papers met the top-tier journal bar (NEJM, Lancet, JAMA, Nature, Cell, Science, Nat Med, Circulation, JACC, Eur Heart J, JCI, Cell Metab, Nature Metabolism, Nat Cardiovasc Res, Circ Res, Lancet Diabetes Endocrinol) in the last 5 days. **Dual-functional poly(disulfide) enables "once-and-for-all" atherosclerosis therapy via precision hepatocyte base editing**
Zhang X, Gao S, Chen D, Wang H, Zuo M, Wang L, et al. *Biomaterials*, 2026.
[PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42214211/) · [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.biomaterials.2026.124333)

A delivery-chemistry paper addressing the practical bottleneck in in vivo base editing — getting the editor into liver cells and out of the endosome. The authors describe a galactose-decorated polymer that engages the asialoglycoprotein receptor for hepatocyte targeting, with a backbone chemistry intended to release cargo directly into the cytosol. In a mouse atherosclerosis model, a single dose produced durable editing at the ANGPTL3 locus, sustained LDL-cholesterol reduction, and less plaque than controls. This is early preclinical work in mice, but it is relevant to anyone tracking whether one-time ANGPTL3 inactivation can compete with the repeat-dosing RNAi and antisense drugs already in the clinic.

**Precision genome editing strategies for enduring lipid lowering in atherosclerosis**
Chowdhury D, Singh N, Garai S, Pandey DP, Bodakhe SH. *European Journal of Pharmacology*, 2026. (Review)
[PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42692283/) · [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ejphar.2026.179312)

A companion-piece review surveying where CRISPR-Cas9, base editing, and prime editing sit for lipid lowering, with PCSK9 and ANGPTL3 as the two anchor targets. The authors frame the field's shift away from crude knockouts toward precise single-nucleotide engineering that avoids double-strand breaks. They also cover the preclinical modeling gap — the case for humanized models that better reflect population genetics — and are candid that delivery (AAV versus lipid nanoparticle) and off-target assessment remain the rate-limiting steps to clinical translation. Useful context, though it does not add new data.

**Genetic susceptibility versus fibrosis progression in North Indian MASLD: distinct roles of APOC3 and PNPLA3**
Tomar N, Choudhury S, Arora A, Sharma P, Vaibhav R, Hasan R, et al. *Journal of Clinical and Experimental Hepatology*, 2026.
[PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42756632/) · [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jceh.2026.103638)

A small candidate-gene study (69 North Indian MASLD patients, 12 SNPs) that separates variants associated with having the disease from variants associated with how far it progresses. PNPLA3 rs738409-G was the strongest within-cohort predictor of significant fibrosis (allelic OR 2.89), with carriers showing markedly higher liver stiffness. APOC3 rs2854116-T was the only variant significantly enriched relative to the Indian population reference after Bonferroni correction (64.0% vs 47.9%; OR 1.93), yet showed no association with fibrosis at all — the authors read this as a susceptibility rather than progression signal. The cohort is small and exploratory, so treat it as hypothesis-generating, but it is a reminder that the APOC3 axis has ancestry-specific frequency differences worth keeping in mind for trial design.

**Barrier identification and strategic design for hypertriglyceridemia management in the post-ACS population at a large integrated health system**
Salvati ZM, Walters NL, Brangan A, Keiser B, Johns A, Voyce SJ, et al. *American Heart Journal Plus*, 2026.
[PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42775079/) · [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ahjo.2026.100897)

An implementation-science study with a sobering result for anyone assuming that better drugs fix the triglyceride care gap. The team surveyed and interviewed clinicians at a large health system, co-designed a strategy package to standardize hypertriglyceridemia management after acute coronary syndrome, and piloted it in one cardiology clinic. Clinicians said a standardized approach was acceptable and feasible and generally favored icosapent ethyl, but flagged coverage concerns and, more bluntly, that hypertriglyceridemia simply is not a priority in the post-ACS visit. After the pilot, only 7.5% used the new resources and just 1.1% of eligible patients received icosapent ethyl — almost all with TG above 500 mg/dL. Education alone did not move prescribing.

**Radiomics from T2-weighted MRI combined with clinical variables for predicting severity in hypertriglyceridemic acute pancreatitis**
Li Y, Wang Y, Wan X, Huang X. *Abdominal Radiology*, 2026.
[PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41925844/) · [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00261-026-05487-0)

A retrospective model-building study in 207 patients with hypertriglyceridemic acute pancreatitis, 48 of whom progressed to severe disease by revised Atlanta criteria, plus a 55-patient external validation cohort from a second campus. A random-forest model combining six radiomic features from the pancreas on T2-weighted MRI with four clinical variables reached AUCs of roughly 0.95 internally and 0.93 externally, outperforming radiomics alone, clinical variables alone, and the BISAP and MRSI scores. Precision-recall analysis held up in the imbalanced dataset. It is single-group, retrospective, and needs prospective testing, but early triage of who will progress remains a real unmet need in SHTG-driven pancreatitis.

## Part B — Industry news (SHTG / Arrowhead / Alnylam / Ionis)

**Roche and Ionis's sefaxersen hits its Phase 3 interim endpoint in IgA nephropathy**
*BioPharma Dive*, Sept 23, 2026 (Jonathan Gardner) — [article](https://www.biopharmadive.com/news/roche-ionis-rna-study-iga-nephropathy/831152/)
See also: *Fierce Biotech*, Sept 23, 2026 (Nick Paul Taylor) — [article](https://www.fiercebiotech.com/biotech/roches-phase-3-win-tees-fight-novartis-blockbuster-kidney-disease-market)

Roche and Ionis reported that sefaxersen, an antisense oligonucleotide that suppresses production of complement factor B, met its primary endpoint at a prespecified Week 37 interim analysis of the Phase 3 IMAGINATION trial, with a statistically significant reduction in proteinuria (24-hour urine protein-to-creatinine ratio) versus placebo. The companies did not release specific numbers; data will be presented at an upcoming meeting and shared with regulators, and the trial stays blinded to assess kidney function at Week 105. Safety and tolerability were described as consistent with earlier trials. The drug is dosed monthly by subcutaneous injection, versus twice-daily oral dosing for Novartis's Fabhalta, which hits the same target. Roche has put peak sales at €1–2 billion against a consensus analyst figure closer to €400 million; Ionis is due up to $400 million in milestones plus royalties.

*Why it matters for SHTG / lipid programs:* Not a lipid story, but it is a balance-sheet and sentiment story for Ionis, which has taken repeated hits this year (including the pelacarsen Lp(a) failure covered in an earlier episode). A partnered win strengthens the platform and the cash position behind the Tryngolza sHTG launch.

No new Arrowhead, Alnylam, or SHTG-specific stories surfaced in the allowed publications within the Sept 20–25 window. The most recent substantive SHTG items — Arrowhead's SHASTA-3/4 plozasiran Phase 3 wins, Redemplo's FDA approval in FCS, Ionis's Tryngolza sHTG approval in June and its subsequent price cut — all predate this cycle and have been covered previously.

## What to watch

Both segments point the same direction: the near-term SHTG catalysts are regulatory and commercial rather than scientific. Arrowhead's plozasiran sHTG filing off the SHASTA-3/4 data has still not landed, and Ionis's next Tryngolza sHTG launch numbers come with Q3 earnings. On the science side, the base-editing delivery work is worth tracking as the durability argument against repeat-dose ANGPTL3 knockdown builds, while the post-ACS implementation study is a useful caution that even approved, effective triglyceride drugs do not prescribe themselves.
