[fiercepharma.com](https://www.fiercepharma.com/pharma/ionis-slashes-tryngolzas-price-tag-down-ahead-anticipated-expansion-wider-shtg-market)
Ionis cut Tryngolza's (olezarsen) annual wholesale acquisition cost from $595,000 — its original FCS list price — to $40,000, effective April 1, applying to both the current FCS indication and the anticipated severe hypertriglyceridemia indication. The company framed the move as aligning with April payer-contracting cycles to secure a "first-mover advantage" ahead of a broad sHTG launch. The $40,000 price undercuts Arrowhead's competing Redemplo (plozasiran), which lists at $60,000 annually. The FDA is expected to decide on olezarsen's sHTG application by June 30, 2026, under priority review.
*Why it matters for SHTG / lipid programs:* The pricing reset sets up the commercial battle lines for the first apoC-III–targeted therapies entering the much larger sHTG market, with the June 30 FDA action the next hard catalyst.

## What to watch

The near-term story is regulatory, not literary: the FDA's expected June 30 decision on olezarsen in severe hypertriglyceridemia would mark the first approved therapy aimed at cutting acute-pancreatitis risk in this population and would crystallize the Ionis–Arrowhead commercial contest already foreshadowed by the Tryngolza/Redemplo price gap. On the science side, watch for the next wave of Phase 3 readouts on plozasiran's SHASTA program (primary completions estimated this month) and any peer-reviewed follow-up analyses from the olezarsen and plozasiran pivotal datasets.
