## Part B — Industry news (SHTG / Arrowhead / Alnylam / Ionis)

**Arrowhead hits "best-case scenario" with latest plozasiran results in severe hypertriglyceridemia**
BioPharma Dive, July 22, 2026 — https://www.biopharmadive.com/news/arrowhead-plozasiran-results-triglyceride-severe-data-trials/825909/

Arrowhead Pharmaceuticals reported positive results from two Phase 3 trials of plozasiran (sold as Redemplo for familial chylomicronemia syndrome) in adults with severe hypertriglyceridemia. Across the two studies, a once-every-three-months injection cut median triglyceride levels by 79% and 81% at one year, versus roughly 27% in placebo arms, and a pooled analysis showed a 78% reduction in acute pancreatitis events. Safety looked "favorable," with no clinically meaningful liver enzyme changes and no low-platelet or hypersensitivity signals — a possible point of differentiation versus Tryngolza, which can raise liver fat. Arrowhead's stock jumped 23% on the news, and the company plans to file for FDA approval in SHTG by year-end, setting up a direct commercial race with Ionis. See also: STAT's Readout newsletter (July 23, 2026) covered the same readout in its daily biotech roundup.

*Why it matters for SHTG / lipid programs:* This is the clearest evidence yet that Arrowhead can go head-to-head with Ionis's Tryngolza in the broader (non-FCS) SHTG market — the pancreatitis benefit in particular strengthens the case that RNAi and ASO approaches targeting APOC3 are converging on comparable efficacy, with dosing convenience (quarterly vs. monthly) becoming a key commercial lever.
