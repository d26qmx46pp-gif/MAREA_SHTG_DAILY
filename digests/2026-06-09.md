## Part A — SHTG / lipid science (PubMed)

**A quarter-century of progress: innovations in lipid medicine that transformed cardiovascular prevention**
Masana L, Ribalta J, Ibarretxe D.
*The Lancet Diabetes & Endocrinology* | Published June 8, 2026
[PMID 42259344](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42259344/) | [DOI 10.1016/S2213-8587(26)00102-6](https://doi.org/10.1016/S2213-8587(26)00102-6)

This invited TimeCapsule review surveys the major transformations in lipid metabolism science and cardiovascular prevention across the first quarter of the 21st century. Starting from the late-20th-century paradigm of statins as near-exclusive therapy, the article traces the clinical and scientific rationale for adding non-statin agents (ezetimibe, PCSK9 inhibitors, bempedoic acid) and then moving into a new era of biologic and RNA/DNA-targeted approaches targeting PCSK9, lipoprotein(a), apolipoprotein C-III, and ANGPTL3. The review explicitly acknowledges the paradigm shift from "LDL only" to a broader residual-risk framework that now incorporates non-HDL cholesterol, apolipoprotein B, triglyceride-rich remnants, and remnant cholesterol — metrics with direct relevance to SHTG. The authors also highlight the challenge to the "HDL is good" dogma and note the growing recognition of Lp(a) as a causal cardiovascular risk factor. A final section addresses the persistent evidence-practice gap — most patients still do not achieve recommended lipid targets — and points to implementation science and artificial intelligence as complementary tools for closing that gap. While this is a narrative review rather than original trial data, its appearance in a top-tier journal on the eve of two pivotal APOC3-targeting regulatory decisions (Ionis sHTG PDUFA June 30; Arrowhead sHTG readout expected mid-2026) makes it useful framing for the field.

## Part B — Industry news (SHTG / Arrowhead / Alnylam / Ionis)

**Ionis appoints Ludwig Hantson, Ph.D. to Board of Directors**
*Ionis Pharmaceuticals press release* | June 8, 2026
[Source (SEC 8-K)](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0000874015/000114036126024407/ef20075669_ex99-1.htm)

Ionis named Ludwig Hantson — former CEO of Alexion and Baxalta — to its Board of Directors, effective immediately. The announcement explicitly frames the appointment in the context of Ionis preparing for "a steady cadence of new commercial launches, including two additional independent launches this year." Those two launches are olezarsen for sHTG and zilganersen for Alexander disease, both assuming regulatory approval. Hantson's background leading rare-disease commercialization at Alexion through the soliris era gives Ionis board-level expertise at a pivotal commercial moment. While a governance appointment is light news for the SHTG field, the framing confirms Ionis views the sHTG olezarsen launch as a near-term priority. The FDA PDUFA date for the sHTG supplemental NDA remains **June 30, 2026**.

*Why it matters for SHTG / lipid programs:* Ionis is staffing its board to support an anticipated commercial expansion into the large sHTG market; approval three weeks out would be the most consequential APOC3 regulatory event since the FCS approvals.

## What to watch

The central event on the horizon is the FDA's June 30 PDUFA date for olezarsen in severe hypertriglyceridemia — a decision that would, if positive, mark the first U.S. approval of an APOC3 inhibitor for a broad, non-genetic triglyceride disorder and open a commercially significant new indication for Ionis. A positive outcome will also sharpen the competitive calculus with Arrowhead, whose SHASTA-4 Phase 3 plozasiran data in sHTG are expected any week. Taken together with today's Lancet Diabetes & Endocrinology review — which traces 25 years of lipid medicine to arrive precisely at APOC3 and ANGPTL3 targeting as the next frontier — the field is entering what looks like a decisive regulatory sprint for RNA-targeted triglyceride therapies.
