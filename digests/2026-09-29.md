**Comparative Efficacy of Insulin and Alternative Therapies for Hypertriglyceridemia-Associated Acute Pancreatitis: A Systematic Review and Network Meta-Analysis**
Liang AC, Castillo YC, Bruciaga LMM, Murga O, Patel AA, Monar GF, et al. *Digestive Diseases and Sciences*, 2026.
[PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42301369/) · [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1007/s10620-026-10052-2)

This PROSPERO-registered network meta-analysis compares the acute TG-lowering strategies used in HTG pancreatitis: insulin, therapeutic plasma exchange (TPE), heparin, hemofiltration and conservative care. Across the evidence, which is mostly observational, no intervention beat insulin-based therapy on mortality, organ failure or length of stay. In the RCT-only analysis, TPE lowered TG more than insulin (mean difference −620 mg/dL, p=0.03), and insulin plus heparin shortened hospital stay by 1.6 days. Faster biochemical TG lowering did not consistently lead to better clinical outcomes, and the certainty of evidence was mostly low or very low. This is useful context for the TG-trajectory data covered Monday. How fast TG falls may mark severity without proving that pushing it down faster in the acute phase changes outcomes. The case for SHTG drugs still rests on preventing pancreatitis, not rescuing an attack in progress.

**Integrating 2.5D multi-modal CT imaging with serum triglycerides for early risk stratification in hypertriglyceridemia-induced acute pancreatitis: A multicenter study**
Guo Q, Chen W, Qin K, Li H, Tang F, Zhou S, et al. *European Journal of Radiology*, 2026.
[PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42322722/) · [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ejrad.2026.113028)

This retrospective study covers 1,518 HTG pancreatitis patients from three hospitals: 990 for training and two external validation cohorts of 340 and 188. A deep-learning model trained on non-contrast and contrast CT taken within 24 hours of admission predicted severe acute pancreatitis with an AUC of 0.89–0.92 in external validation. Adding a single serum TG value ("Fusion-TG") raised the AUC to about 0.97. It also raised sensitivity for severe disease from 60.6% to 94.4% in one external cohort while keeping specificity high. In SHAP analysis, the dominant contributors were contrast-CT features and TG. It is retrospective and from a single country, but the size and external validation make it stronger than most HTG pancreatitis prediction papers. It also supports treating admission TG as a severity signal, not just a diagnostic label.

**Fresh frozen plasma as functional replacement therapy in early infantile ApoC-II deficiency: a molecularly confirmed case series**
Ergun NÜ, Aslantaş M. *Journal of Pediatric Endocrinology & Metabolism*, 2026.
[PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42148757/) · [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1515/jpem-2026-0123)

The authors report three male infants from consanguineous families, all homozygous for the pathogenic APOC2 splice variant c.55+1G>C, with TG of 3,228–5,802 mg/dL. A fat-restricted diet alone did not lower TG fast enough. Fresh frozen plasma (10 mL/kg twice daily for 3 days) cut TG by up to 98.3% within 72 hours, and lactescent serum, vomiting and eruptive xanthomas resolved. Plasma works here because it supplies the missing LPL cofactor, and the brisk response also supports the diagnosis. This is a small case series, but it is a clean demonstration of the biology: replacing ApoC-II restores LPL function almost immediately. It also shows the edge of the FCS genotype spectrum, where APOC3 silencing is not the obvious first move.

**The 2026 ACC/AHA Dyslipidemia Guideline: A Critical and Transatlantic Perspective on Personalized Lipid Management**
Sabouret P, Mafrica D, Giamundo DM, Kalisz JP, Asher E, Atar D. *Life (Basel)*, 2026.
[PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42795336/) · [DOI](https://doi.org/10.3390/life16091470)

This is a review, not primary data. It critiques the 2026 ACC/AHA multisociety guideline, which expands the LDL-C-centered approach to cover non-HDL-C, ApoB, Lp(a), triglyceride-rich lipoproteins, inherited lipid disorders and severe hypertriglyceridemia. It also notes the guideline's use of PREVENT-ASCVD and its return to explicit lipid targets. The authors separate recommendations backed by outcome trials from those that rest on observational data, surrogate endpoints or extrapolation, which is a relevant distinction for TRL- and SHTG-directed therapy. They also compare the guideline with the 2025 ESC/EAS Focused Update. It is worth a skim if you want an outside read on how the new US guideline handles SHTG.

ADARx priced 26 million-plus shares at $17, the top of its $15–17 range, for a $446.3M IPO. It also raised about $89M through a private placement with partner AbbVie, and it trades on Nasdaq as ADRX. BioPharma Dive calls it the first RNA-focused biotech IPO in almost two years and the first traditional US IPO from an RNAi drugmaker in more than a decade. Its lead drug, onvuzosiran, is a twice-yearly siRNA against plasma kallikrein in Phase 3 for hereditary angioedema, where it will compete with Ionis's approved HAE drug. Phase 3 data are expected by the end of 2027. In its filing, ADARx says RNAi is still "constrained" by delivery and potency. Its pipeline goes beyond the liver, including obesity and neurology programs.
*Why it matters for SHTG / lipid programs:* This is a platform and financing signal, not lipid news. Public markets are paying up again for siRNA companies, which supports valuations for the space Arrowhead, Alnylam and Ionis define. The push beyond the liver matters less for APOC3/ANGPTL3, which are liver targets that current GalNAc chemistry already reaches.

## What to watch

The science this cycle keeps returning to acute HTG pancreatitis. Admission TG and how fast it clears predict severity, but the meta-analysis says faster acute lowering has not been shown to change outcomes. That supports prevention (keeping TG low chronically with APOC3/ANGPTL3 silencing) as the lever that matters, the argument behind the 78% pancreatitis reduction Arrowhead reported with plozasiran in SHASTA-3/4 this summer. On the industry side, watch for Arrowhead's sHTG sNDA timing, early Tryngolza sHTG launch metrics in Ionis's Q3 report, and any update on the Ionis–Arrowhead APOC3 patent litigation.
