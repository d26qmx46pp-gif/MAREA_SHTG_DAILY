# ANGPTL4 & Adipose RNA — Monday September 7

**Bottom line:** A thin week for new science — one paper adds ANGPTL4 to the growing list of endometriosis biomarkers, and one describes an adipose-targeted lipid nanoparticle (not RNA-loaded, but relevant delivery-platform work) for reprogramming obese adipose tissue immunity.

## Papers

**Evaluation of angiopoietin-like protein 4 levels in patients with endometriosis**
Eşkin Tanrıverdi, Erol Koç — *Journal of the Turkish German Gynecological Association* (2026)
[PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42694335/) | [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1210/clinem/dgaa513)

A prospective case-control study measured serum and peritoneal fluid ANGPTL4 in women with surgically confirmed endometriosis versus age-matched healthy controls. Serum ANGPTL4 was roughly three times higher in the endometriosis group (512.65 vs. 177.60 ng/mL, p<0.001), tracking alongside elevated CA-125, and correlated positively with pelvic pain severity. The authors frame this as support for ANGPTL4's role in the angiogenic and inflammatory processes that drive endometriosis, and propose it as a candidate adjunct biomarker in a disease that still lacks a definitive blood test. This is a small, single-center, correlative study — it doesn't establish causality or mechanism, and ANGPTL4 elevation is already documented across multiple inflammatory and metabolic conditions, so its diagnostic specificity here remains unproven.

**Interleukin-4-Loaded Lipid Nanoparticles Reprogram Adipose Tissue Immunity and Promote Adipocyte Browning for the Treatment of Obesity**
Mohaghegh, Balajam, Kraemer, Marx, Sawant, Digholkar, et al. — *Acta Biomaterialia* (2026)
[PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42692230/) | [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.actbio.2026.09.007)

This is a delivery-platform paper rather than an RNA therapeutic, but it's directly relevant to the adipose-tropic LNP space we track. The group engineered IL-4-loaded lipid nanoparticles (~150 nm, >70% encapsulation efficiency) via thin-film hydration/extrusion to sustain local IL-4 delivery to macrophages in inflamed white adipose tissue. In vitro, IL-4/LNP outperformed free IL-4 at reprogramming pro-inflammatory M1 macrophages toward the anti-inflammatory M2 phenotype (CD206 up, CD80/CD40 down, less TNF-α/IL-6), and paracrine signaling in adipocyte-macrophage co-cultures promoted browning of white and brown adipose-mimicking cells. The core takeaway for our beat: another concrete example of LNP engineering being tuned specifically for adipose tissue tropism and macrophage uptake, a formulation problem that overlaps directly with adipose-targeted siRNA/ASO delivery even though the payload here is a cytokine, not RNA.

## Research Integrity Note

A 2025 *International Journal of Biological Macromolecules* paper claiming ANGPTL4 promotes hepatocellular carcinoma progression via M2 macrophage polarization ("RNA-seq shows angiopoietin-like 4 promotes hepatocellular carcinoma progression...") was retracted this week ([PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42486762/)). No reason was given in the retraction notice itself. Worth flagging if this paper has been cited in any internal ANGPTL4-oncology literature reviews.

## What to watch
