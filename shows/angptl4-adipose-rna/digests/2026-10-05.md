# ANGPTL4 & Adipose RNA — Monday October 5

**Bottom line:** A modest week: a mechanistic paper shows DHA raises adipocyte Angptl4 via PPARγ to suppress LPL, a sarcopenia atlas casts adipose-derived ANGPTL4 as a paracrine brake on muscle regeneration, and a J Control Release review places adipose RNAi in Phase 1/2a among extrahepatic delivery platforms.

## Papers

**1. DHA increases Angptl4 gene expression and reduces LPL activity in a PPARγ-dependent manner in adipocytes**
McTavish PV, Rajna A, Brown LH, Elzaanoun R, et al. *Biochim Biophys Acta Mol Cell Biol Lipids* (Sep 30, 2026).
[PMID 42815684](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42815684/) · [DOI 10.1016/j.bbalip.2026.159774](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.bbalip.2026.159774)
Re-examining a prior mouse study, the authors found that an EPA/DHA-rich diet raised fasted white-adipose Angptl4 expression. In 3T3-L1 adipocytes, DHA (100 μM) — but not ALA or EPA — increased Angptl4 and lowered LPL activity, mirroring the PPARγ agonist pioglitazone. siRNA knockdown of Pparγ abolished DHA- and pioglitazone-induced Angptl4 induction, and silencing Angptl4 blunted the drop in LPL activity. This suggests omega-3 TG lowering may not be purely hepatic and could partly involve adipose ANGPTL4 rerouting TG away from fat; relevant context for anyone thinking about ANGPTL4 inhibition alongside omega-3 or PPARγ therapy. In vitro only; in vivo confirmation is pending.

**2. Single-Nucleus Atlas of Spinal Sarcopenia Patients Reveals Disruption of Quiescent and Regenerative Satellite Cell Niches in Fatty-Infiltrated Muscle**
Wu W, Wang R, Wang D, Ren Q, et al. *Advanced Science* (Oct 4, 2026).
[PMID 42829920](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42829920/) · [DOI 10.1002/advs.77648](https://doi.org/10.1002/advs.77648)
In patients with spinal sarcopenia, intramuscular (not perimuscular) fat fraction independently predicted lower trunk muscle mass and strength. Single-nucleus RNA-seq of fat-infiltrated paraspinal muscle showed remodeling of satellite-cell niches and a GPC3-high, lipogenic fibro/adipogenic progenitor population. Among adipose-derived factors, ANGPTL4 emerged as a paracrine suppressor of myogenesis acting through syndecan-2 upregulation in satellite cells; SDC2 inhibition partially rescued ANGPTL4-induced impairment in C2C12 cells. This is a non-lipid ANGPTL4 activity worth tracking — it hints that lowering ANGPTL4 might be neutral-to-favorable for muscle, though the functional evidence is cell-line only.

**3. Delivery architectures for oligonucleotide therapeutics in genetic and rare diseases: GalNAc-based hepatocyte targeting and extrahepatic translation** *(Review)*
Lee CH, Moon H, Lee KH, Na JH, et al. *Journal of Controlled Release* (Oct 3, 2026).
[PMID 42829049](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42829049/) · [DOI 10.1016/j.jconrel.2026.115431](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jconrel.2026.115431)
Uses GalNAc-siRNA (seven FDA-approved drugs as of July 18, 2026) as the reference model for how ligand, linker, chemistry and PK/PD enable durable subcutaneous hepatocyte pharmacology. It then compares extrahepatic routes — CNS, muscle, eye, lung, kidney, immune cells and adipose. The authors classify adipose RNAi as in Phase 1/2a, alongside intrathecal C16-siRNA and TfR1-antibody conjugates as the more advanced extrahepatic approaches. A useful landscape reference for positioning adipose-directed siRNA; it is a review, not new data.

## What to watch

The DHA paper raises a testable question: does omega-3 therapy's TG-lowering partly depend on adipose ANGPTL4, and would ANGPTL4 inhibition blunt or add to it? In vivo adipose-specific knockout data would settle it. On the delivery side, watch for first clinical readouts from the adipose RNAi programs the JCR review flags as Phase 1/2a — they will be the first human test of whether tissue-tropic siRNA can reach fat at therapeutic levels. The muscle-ANGPTL4–SDC2 link merits follow-up in primary human satellite cells.
