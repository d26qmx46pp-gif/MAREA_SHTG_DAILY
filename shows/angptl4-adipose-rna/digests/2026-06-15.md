**FTO Regulates Porcine Adipogenesis and Postnatal Survival of Cloned Embryos**
Wang X, Zhang M, Yang Z, Hu K, Zhu R, et al. | *FASEB Journal* | 2026
PMID: [42257483](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42257483/) | DOI: [10.1096/fj.202504686RR](https://doi.org/10.1096/fj.202504686RR)

FTO (fat mass and obesity-associated gene) is the first identified obesity-related gene and functions as an m6A RNA demethylase — but its role in large mammals has been poorly characterized. This study used siRNA-mediated knockdown of FTO in porcine adipose-derived mesenchymal stem cells (ADSCs) combined with transcriptome sequencing to probe its role in adipogenesis. FTO silencing suppressed differentiation into mature adipocytes by downregulating key adipogenic transcription factors (PPARγ, C/EBPα) and lipogenic genes (FABP4, SCD, LPL, ACCα). Separate CRISPR/Cas9 FTO-knockout experiments revealed disrupted cell cycle, senescence, and metabolic pathways (PI3K-Akt, Wnt, MAPK), and cloned piglets carrying the knockout died postnatally — pointing to FTO as essential for porcine fat development and survival. For the RNA-delivery field, this work validates siRNA knockdown in porcine ADSCs as an effective functional genomics tool in a large-mammal adipose model, a step closer to clinical translation than rodent systems.

**Gengnianshou Formula Inhibits Estrogen Deficiency–Induced Obesity via an HSPA5-Associated Phosphatidylcholine Regulatory Pathway**
Zhang W, Zhang W-T, Fan J-X, Wang Y-Y, Kang M-J, et al. | *Journal of Ethnopharmacology* | 2026
PMID: [42178075](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42178075/) | DOI: [10.1016/j.jep.2026.121886](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jep.2026.121886)

This study investigates a traditional Chinese medicine formula (Gengnianshou/GNS) for perimenopausal obesity using an ovariectomized rat model, network pharmacology, transcriptomics, and lipidomics. A critical mechanistic experiment uses siRNA-mediated knockdown of HSPA5 (an ER chaperone) in human adipose-derived stem cells (hADSCs) under estrogen-deprived conditions to validate that HSPA5 is a functional mediator of the formula's anti-obesity effects, acting through phosphatidylcholine metabolism. While the primary topic is traditional medicine, the paper's use of siRNA knockdown in hADSCs to functionally validate a metabolic target in human adipose progenitors is directly relevant to the adipose RNA delivery field. The phosphatidylcholine pathway HSPA5 governs also warrants attention for lipid nanoparticle formulation, where PC is a key structural component.

## What to Watch

The ANGPTL4 biomarker correspondence from Taiwan keeps the vascular permeability angle in view: if serum ANGPTL4 reliably predicts hemorrhagic transformation after stroke therapy, that raises both opportunity and safety considerations for any ANGPTL4-lowering therapeutic. Two independent groups this week chose siRNA as the tool of choice to interrogate adipose biology — in porcine ADSCs (FTO) and human hADSCs (HSPA5) — reinforcing the accessibility of siRNA in adipose progenitor cell models. The porcine ADSC system is worth watching as a large-mammal platform for testing adipose-tropic RNA delivery vehicles ahead of non-human primate studies.
