# ANGPTL4 & Adipose RNA — Monday July 6

**Bottom line:** Three new mechanistic papers this week converge on ANGPTL4 as a secreted signal that drives fibrosis and immune evasion outside its classic lipid-metabolism role — in kidney, liver tumor stroma, and now the blood-brain barrier — while no new adipose-targeted RNA-delivery papers or patents cleared the bar.

## Papers

**Renal tubular epithelial-derived Angptl4 promotes renal fibrosis by inducing tubular cell senescence.**
Huanan Li, Zifan Zhu, Peifen Li, Qiaojun Shen, Yihao Wu, et al. *Biochemical and Biophysical Research Communications* (2026).
[PubMed: 42289177](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42289177) | [DOI: 10.1016/j.bbrc.2026.154133](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.bbrc.2026.154133)

The authors found ANGPTL4 upregulated in kidney tubular cells of chronic kidney disease (CKD) patients and in fibrotic and aging mouse kidneys. Knocking out Angptl4 in tubular cells reduced both fibrosis and cellular senescence, while overexpressing it made both worse. Mechanistically, tubular-derived Angptl4 binds integrin β1, activates focal adhesion kinase, and pushes tubular cells into a senescence-associated secretory state that in turn activates fibroblasts to lay down scar tissue. It's a clean addition to the growing literature on ANGPTL4 as a driver of fibrotic disease beyond its lipid-metabolism role, and it nominates the Angptl4–integrin β1–FAK axis as a potential target for CKD.

**Cancer-Associated Fibroblasts Promote Tumor Immunosuppression in Hepatocellular Carcinoma via the NNMT-ANGPTL4 Axis.**
Shounan Lu, Shanjia Ke, Hongjun Yu, Zhanzhi Meng, Miaoyu Bai, et al. *Advanced Science* (2026).
[PubMed: 41980209](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41980209) | [DOI: 10.1002/advs.202521418](https://doi.org/10.1002/advs.202521418)

This one traces a metabolic-epigenetic cascade in cancer-associated fibroblasts: high NNMT expression sequesters EZH2 out of the nucleus, which lowers a repressive histone mark at the ANGPTL4 promoter and boosts ANGPTL4 secretion. Secreted ANGPTL4 then engages GLUT1 on hepatocellular carcinoma cells, ramping up glycolysis and a histone lactylation mark that turns on PD-L1 — helping the tumor evade the immune system. ANGPTL4 also promoted angiogenesis in this model. Targeting the NNMT-ANGPTL4 axis restored anti-tumor CD8 T-cell activity in the paper's therapeutic experiments, adding hepatocellular carcinoma to the list of settings where ANGPTL4 blockade has a plausible immuno-oncology rationale.

**The pyroptosis cascade between inflammatory endothelial cells and microglia facilitates BBB disruption in bacterial meningitis.**
Yangyang Zheng, Zhiyue Wu, Fei Wu, Hanyu Hu, Jiaxiu Ma, et al. *Cell Reports* (2026).
[PubMed: 42378091](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42378091) | [DOI: 10.1016/j.celrep.2026.117634](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.celrep.2026.117634)

Using neonatal meningitis-causing E. coli infection models, the authors show that brain endothelial cells undergo GSDMD-dependent pyroptosis, and that this triggers a second wave of pyroptosis in neighboring microglia through Angptl4-Sdc4 signaling between the two cell types. This "pyroptosis cascade" is proposed as a mechanism for blood-brain-barrier breakdown in bacterial meningitis. It's a novel context for ANGPTL4 — not metabolic or fibrotic, but as an intercellular alarm signal in acute CNS inflammation — and worth tracking for anyone mapping the full biology of this protein outside adipose tissue.

## What to watch

All three papers this week reinforce ANGPTL4's expanding identity as a tissue-damage signal — in kidney fibrosis, liver tumor immune evasion, and now CNS blood-brain-barrier breakdown — rather than a straightforward lipid-metabolism regulator. None of them touch adipose-targeted RNA delivery directly, and this week's PubMed pull turned up no siRNA/ASO/LNP papers specific to adipose tissue; the RNA-delivery side of this beat has been quiet for two consecutive cycles. Worth watching for the NNMT-ANGPTL4 axis in particular, since a druggable upstream node (NNMT) paired with a secreted downstream effector (ANGPTL4) is exactly the kind of setup that tends to attract therapeutic programs.
