## What to watch

The recent run of ANGPTL4 papers has clustered around tumor-microenvironment biology — macrophage-derived ANGPTL4 in liver cancer metastasis, ANGPTL4's role in dampening CD8+ T-cell infiltration, and its pro-fibrotic signaling in the kidney — rather than metabolic or cardiovascular applications closer to Marea's core interest. Nothing new on adipose-tropic siRNA/ASO/LNP delivery platforms has surfaced in the last two search cycles. Worth keeping an eye on: any follow-up mechanistic work connecting ANGPTL4's integrin-signaling axis (integrin α5β1/β1, seen in both the cholangiocarcinoma and renal fibrosis papers) to metabolic tissues, and any GalNAc- or LNP-based delivery papers specifically targeting adipocytes, which have been notably absent from recent search cycles.
