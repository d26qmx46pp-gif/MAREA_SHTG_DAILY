## Part A — Acromegaly research

*No top-tier-journal (NEJM, Lancet, JAMA, etc.) acromegaly papers appeared this week; showing next-best tier.*

**Determinant factors for headache at acromegaly diagnosis: findings from an Iberian multicenter study encompassing 726 patients**
Lopes, de Griné Severino, Nobre et al. — *J Endocrinol Invest*, Nov 2026. [PMID 42573676](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42573676/) · [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1007/s40618-026-03016-w)
In a registry of 726 patients from 35 Iberian centers, 44.2% had headache at diagnosis. Those patients were younger and more often had macroadenomas, larger and more invasive tumors, hypopituitarism and hyperprolactinemia. In multivariable analysis, macroadenoma and larger tumor volume remained the independent determinants, making tumor size the dominant factor.

**Association between pathological subtypes of PitNETs and postoperative biochemical remission in acromegaly**
Li, Luo, Zhang et al. — *Endocrine*, Oct 31 2026. [PMID 42965779](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42965779/) · [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1007/s12020-026-04856-5)
Retrospective surgical cohort (2015–2025), overall remission 51.0%. After propensity weighting, neither transcription-factor-based nor hormone-based pure vs non-pure subtypes predicted postoperative remission. Higher preoperative IGF-1 index was a negative predictor in one classification only.

**Liver steatosis and fibrosis in acromegaly patients with active and controlled disease: a scoping review**
Sagan, Lipiec-Rzepecka, Patalong-Wójcik et al. — *J Endocrinol Invest*, Nov 2026. [PMID 42240753](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42240753/) · [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1007/s40618-026-02940-1)
Twenty-one studies, highly heterogeneous. The pattern: lower intrahepatic lipid in active acromegaly but a higher likelihood of fibrosis versus controls, with lipid content possibly rising once disease is controlled. Authors call for higher-quality studies.

**Prostatic enlargement in acromegaly despite preserved lower urinary tract function**
Kaymak, Canat, Coskun et al. — *Prostate*, Nov 1 2026. [PMID 42967319](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42967319/) · [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1002/pros.70274)
Cross-sectional study of 57 men with acromegaly (25 active) and 24 controls. Median prostate volume was larger (30 vs 20 mL) and calcification more frequent (31% vs 5%), with enlargement above 30 mL more common in active than remitted disease. Urinary symptoms, uroflowmetry, mood and quality-of-life scores did not differ.

**Functioning pituitary adenomas in pregnancy: updates and controversies**
Varlamov, Gheorghiu, Fleseriu — *Curr Opin Endocrinol Diabetes Obes*, Dec 2026. [PMID 42506880](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42506880/) · [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1097/MED.0000000000000968)
Review covering prolactinoma, acromegaly and Cushing's in pregnancy. Dopamine agonists show consistent maternal and fetal safety, and surgery is increasingly considered an alternative for selected prolactinoma patients; rarity limits firm evidence.

## Part B — Rare Disease Industry News

**FDA unveils guidance for "plug and play" gene therapies**
BioPharma Dive (Kristin Jensen), Oct 28, 2026 — https://biopharmadive.com/news/fda-personalized-gene-therapy-rare-disease-guidance/873004
Draft guidance would let a sponsor win approval for one mutation traditionally, then extend to other mutations in the same gene using laboratory data rather than a new clinical study, defined in an "individualized feature space" in the original application. Analyst Joseph Thome of TD Cowen flagged Prime Medicine as especially relevant; Prime shares rose 15% and Beam 8% early Wednesday.
*Why it matters for rare disease developers:* a possible faster path to cover many mutations of the same gene without repeating trials.

**With Setara, the team behind Actio opens its second act**
BioPharma Dive (Gwendolyn Wu), Oct 30, 2026 — https://biopharmadive.com/news/setara-actio-charcot-marie-tooth-ion-channel-drugs/873227
Setara Biosciences launched with $90M. Lead STAR-0871 is a TRPV4 ion-channel blocker for severe Charcot-Marie-Tooth type 2C, with a Phase 1b planned by year-end and an overactive bladder Phase 2 also planned. Investors include Deerfield, Canaan and Jazz, which holds a minority stake after buying the team's prior company Actio.
*Why it matters for rare disease developers:* continued investor appetite for ion-channel neuromuscular programs.

**Context piece (Oct 19, outside window): Merck KGaA bets on RNAi with $1.3B Silence buyout**
BioPharma Dive (Jonathan Gardner), Oct 19, 2026 — https://www.biopharmadive.com/news/merck-kgaa-rnai-13b-silence-buyout-blood-disorder-polycythemia-vera/833336/
Merck KGaA agreed to buy Silence Therapeutics for $1.3B, $20 cash per U.S.-listed share (130% premium), closing expected Q1 2027. The asset is divesiran, an siRNA for polycythemia vera with positive Phase 2 data; a Phase 3 is still needed.
*Why it matters for rare disease developers:* big pharma is paying premiums for RNAi assets in rare blood disorders.

## What to watch
Acromegaly evidence this week is incremental, but the FDA's plug-and-play framework, if finalized, could reshape development for genetic rare diseases broadly, and the Merck KGaA deal shows continued appetite for RNAi-based rare disease assets.
